BIOVAGON LABS.

Bioinformatics isn't learned by watching. It's learned by doing. Biovagon Labs is the research and innovation division of Biovagon, a virtual-first environment where students stop attending sessions and start contributing to real bioinformatics work.

→ SEE OPEN ROLES WHY WE BUILT THIS
// why biovagon labs

A LAB, NOT
A CLASSROOM.

Our goal was never to teach bioinformatics.

Our goal is to make you do bioinformatics.

At Biovagon, bioinformatics has always been at the heart of what we do. Workshops and internships are valuable starting points, but they were never meant to be the destination. Most students finish a course with a certificate and little experience of how research actually happens: no project of their own, no team, no paper discussed, no progress presented.

Biovagon Labs closes that gap. Inspired by the culture of a research laboratory, it's a virtual research environment where students don't just attend — they join a team, work on a live project, sit in on journal club, and think through problems alongside mentors and fellow researchers.

We believe research shouldn't be limited by geography. Through Biovagon Labs, anyone with curiosity and commitment can become part of a collaborative research ecosystem — virtually, but not hypothetically.
