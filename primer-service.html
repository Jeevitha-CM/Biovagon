// questions

FAQ.

What do I need to send you?
Your target sequence (FASTA or plain text), the organism, the application (qPCR, conventional PCR, cloning, etc.), and any constraints on product size or primer location. That's it.
How quickly will I get results?
Basic: 48h. Standard: 24h. Premium: 12h. These are maximum turnarounds — most orders are delivered faster.
What if the primers don't work in the lab?
We'll redesign at no charge if the primers fail under the exact conditions described at order time. Wet lab variables outside our control (sample quality, reagent issues) are excluded, but we'll still help you troubleshoot.
Do you do multiplex or degenerate primers?
Yes — contact us before ordering to discuss. Multiplex and degenerate designs are handled under the Premium tier or as custom quotes.
Can I use this for my thesis or publication?
Absolutely. Our reports are formatted for citation and include all the information reviewers typically ask for — sequences, Tm, GC%, BLAST accession numbers.
